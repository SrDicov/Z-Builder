Artix Linux Gaming ISO

Do not launch Steam in live! It downloads assets and may exhaust the little free squashfs space of the live environment.
Same for Lutris and especially the bundled Beyond All Reason RTS game launcher.

Highlights

- Kernels: linux-zen and mainline
- Plasma desktop (XFCE4 variant soon), dinit
- Game launchers: Steam, Heroic Launcher (Epic Games), Lutris and DOSBox
- FS-UAE for Amiga computers emulation
- Most optimizations suggested in the Gaming#Improving_performace section of the Arch wiki
- High-quality virtual MIDI device support with Timidity++ for Linux, Wine and DOSBox
- Various tools and utilities for configuring, tweaking and monitoring your hardware (LACT, Goverlay, MangoHud, GameMode, AntiMicroX, Piper, Solaar, OpenRGB,  etc)
- Almost all software from the community ISOs
- A commercial-grade open-source RTS game, Beyond All Reason

Notes

- NVidia users must manually install the right drivers for their card. The Artix repositories contain the latest nvidia-dkms + the latest legacy nvidia-580xx-dkms packages, along with their assorted utilities and lib32 counterparts.
- Installation takes up over 16GB of disk space
